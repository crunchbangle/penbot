Was having ideas about finding where the page is.

Potentially could mount my camera on the bot. Then you could
place it with at least the camera on the paper, and it could:

1. Find the edge
2. Find a corner
3. Find the next corner
4. Find a third corner and determine the paper geometry
5. Check the 4th corner.
6. Collect data about adjustments/calibration as it goes
    - comparative wheel slippage on paper vs on mat
    - play in the gearbox
    - etc

Then you could give it some text and it should be able to:

1. Put the first line down in a straight line
2. Return to start of second line and double-check distance from first using camera (and maybe orientation using edge of paper!)

This means it could adjust for any drift in angle/position at the start
of every line.

It also occured to me that lined paper would help!
